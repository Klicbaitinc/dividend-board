Where the data comes from

Every entry comes from a Form 8-K (and its press-release exhibits) filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission and found through EDGAR full-text search. A script reads the announcement sentence, decides whether it describes a raise, cut, suspension or new dividend, and pulls out the old and new per-share amounts when the filing states them.

  • Each row links to the original filing, and stock pages quote the exact sentence we used.
  • If we couldn't read an amount automatically, the cell shows "—". We never estimate amounts.
  • Rows marked inferred had no explicit "cut" wording. The newly declared amount was just lower than the company's previous declaration. Check those in the filing.
  • Preferred-stock coupons, special dividends and variable dividends are left out.
  • Companies that announce only by press release, without filing an 8-K, won't show up.
